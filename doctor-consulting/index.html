GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things

Have questions about bookings or procedures? We’re here to help ↗

How do I book an appointment? +

Browse our doctor directory, select the specialty or doctor that matches your needs, choose between a clinic visit or online consultation, and select an available time. Fill out your contact details to receive a confirmed booking instantly.

Can I meet with my doctor online? +

Yes! Many of our certified doctors offer secure video consultations. Simply look for the "Online visits" badge on the doctor's profile and choose "Online consultation" when booking.

How are doctors verified on MediConsult? +

Doctors listed on MediConsult are required to submit valid medical board registrations (e.g. MBBS, FCPS, MCPS, BDS) and license verifications to ensure high standards of patient safety and care quality.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment? +

Your booking reference code is immediately stored in your browser. If you need to make changes or cancel, you can contact the clinic directly using the information provided in your booking confirmation.
